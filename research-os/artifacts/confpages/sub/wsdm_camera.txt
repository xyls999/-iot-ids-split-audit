 Camera Ready Instructions
 Link to the preparation instructions: 
 http://www.scomminc.com/pp/acmsig/wsdm2026.htm  
 Hard Submission Deadline: December 17th 
 To expect two emails: 1 from 
 submissions@scomminc.com  with their submission id# with link to submit/upload their final version –and– 1 from ACM Rightsreview to the lead contact author only for the ACM eform to be completed
 Length of contributions for the proceedings (by track):
 (fp) Full Research Paper  = 9 Page Limit + unlimited pages for references and ethical considerations only
 (sp) Short Paper = 4 Page Limit + unlimited pages for references and ethical considerations only
 (dp) Demo Paper = 4 Page Limit for all content, no extra pages
 (wk) Workshop Abstract = 2 Page Limit for all content, no extra pages 
 (tp) Tutorial Paper = 4 Page Limit for all content, no extra pages
 (dc) Doctoral Consortium = 3 Page Limit for all content, no extra pages 
 (id) Industry Day Abstract = 2 Page Limit for all content, no extra pages 
 (wd) WSDM Day Abstract = 2 Page Limit for all content, no extra pages 
 (kn) Keynote Talk Abstract = 2 Page Limit for all content, no extra pages
