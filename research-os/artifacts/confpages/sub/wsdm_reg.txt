 Registration
 Author Registration Policy
 Paper Presentation Fee
 Each paper in the research (full and short) track and in the demo track accepted for presentation at the conference must be accompanied by a distinct paper presentation fee . The fee is $150 for full research papers (long
 papers) and $75 for short research papers and demo papers (short papers).
 Conference Registration
 For full research/short research/demo papers : At least one author of each accepted paper needs to register for the conference (either 3-Day or 5-Day registration) by December 17, 2025 at the rates below:
 Full research/short research/demo papers without a valid accompanying registration and paper publication fee risk being excluded from the proceedings. 
 One-Day (Sunday Feb. 22 or Thursday Feb. 26) registration
 For all submissions accepted to one-day programs, including Industry Day, Tutorials, Workshops, PhD Symposium, and AnalytiCup, at least one presenting author is expected to register for the required day, either via a “One-Day” registration
 that covers that day (Sunday Feb. 22 or Thursday Feb. 26) or via a “5-Day” registration.
We appreciate your understanding and compliance with these author registration requirements. If you have any questions or require further clarification, please feel free to contact us at WSDM-reg@acm.org
 Registration Fees
 If you are a student, you may register under either category, you will be asked to verify your student status on the registration form. If you are a student member select ACM Member Registration, if you are NOT a member select the Non-ACM Member registration. Please contact WSDM-reg@acm.org if you have any questions. 
 Additional Guest Ticket (for 3-day or 5-Day conference registrations)
 The 3-day Conference Registration covers:
 Attendance at the main conference sessions for three days, including keynotes
 Morning/afternoon coffee on those same says
 Access to the conference proceedings via the ACM Digital Library during the period of the conference
 One ticket to the reception
 One ticket to the banquet
 Box lunch on the third day for the WSDM Town Hall meeting
 [subject to meeting financial targets] Box lunch on first and second day of conference
 The 5-day Conference Registration covers:
 Same as three-day registration
 Attendance at tutorials, workshops, industry day, and, if invited, the PhD Symposium
 Morning/afternoon coffee on Sunday and Thursday
 [subject to meeting financial targets] Box lunch on Sunday and Thursday
 The One-day Conference Registration covers:
 Attendance at conference sessions on the nominated day
 Morning/afternoon coffee on the nominated day
 [subject to meeting financial targets] Box lunch on the nominated day
 Does not include attendance at the reception or banquet 
 Cancellation Policy
 No refunds are allowed. Please note that the registration cannot be canceled; the only allowed exception is to transfer the registration to someone else in case of visa denial. Please consult our attendance policy here: 
 https://wsdm-conference.org/2026/index.php/attendance-policy/ 
 Eligibility for Special Rates Policy
 Student Registration Rates:
 To be eligible for Student Registration rates, attendees must meet the following criteria:
 Be enrolled as full-time students at an accredited educational institution.
 Be able to provide evidence of their full-time student status during registration.
 ACM Member Registration Rates
 To be eligible for ACM Member Registration rates, attendees must meet the following criteria:
 Be current members of ACM (Association for Computing Machinery).
 Be able to provide evidence of their membership status during registration.
 SIGIR and SIGWEB Student Travel Award Winners
 SIGIR (Special Interest Group on Information Retrieval) and SIGWEB (Special Interest Group on Hypertext, Hypermedia, and the Web) Student Travel Award winners will receive complimentary conference registration (main conference and
 workshops/tutorials if desired). If you plan to apply for one of these awards, please refrain from registering for the conference until you receive the award notification.
 We appreciate your cooperation and adherence to these eligibility criteria. If you have any questions or concerns regarding your eligibility or registration status, please contact us for clarification at WSDM-reg@acm.org
