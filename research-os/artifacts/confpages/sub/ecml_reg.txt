 Conditions of Use
 Unless there are exceptional circumstances (such as travel limitations due to conflict), at least one author from each accepted paper must present their work in person at the conference.
 To be eligible for the student discount, you must upload proof of your student status during the registration process. Submitted documents will be reviewed, and confirmation of the discount will be provided once your status has been validated.
 You can request your invitation letter for visa application after you receive the acceptance notification. If you need a visa to travel to Italy, please contact the nearest Italian embassy or consulate in your country. If you have any questions, please contact the local organizers.
 Cancellations received before August 1st, 2026, will be returned in full, minus a 20% administrative charge. Cancellations made after this date will not be refunded.
