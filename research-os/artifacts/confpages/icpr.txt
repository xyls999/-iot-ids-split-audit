 About the ICPR Conference
 The International Conference on Pattern Recognition (ICPR) is
 the leading event of the International Association for Pattern
 Recognition, recognized as a top conference in the field. It
 encompasses a wide range of topics where Pattern Recognition
 methods are applied in fields including Computer Vision,
 Machine Learning, Image Processing, Speech and Natural
 Language Processing, and Sensor Pattern Processing. The 28th
 ICPR, taking place from 17 to 22 August 2026, offers an
 excellent platform for students, academics, and industry
 researchers to foster new ideas and collaborations.
