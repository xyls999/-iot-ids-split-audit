Title: Call for Papers - IROS 2026 | IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems

The 2026 IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS 2026) will be held from September 27 through October 1, 2026 at the David Lawrence Convention Center, Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, USA. IROS is one of the largest and highest-impact robotics research conferences worldwide. It provides an international forum for the international robotics research community to explore frontiers of science and technology in intelligent robots and smart machines. In addition to technical sessions and multi-media presentations, IROS will also include panel discussions, forums, workshops, tutorials, exhibits, and technical tours to enrich the fruitful discussions among conference attendees.

Innovative research results on topics related (but not limited) to the following are invited: robot design, robot kinematics/dynamics/control, system integration, AI in robotics, sensor/actuator networks, distributed and cloud robotics, bio-inspired systems, service robots, robotics in automation, biomedical applications, autonomous vehicles (land, sea, and air), robot perception, manipulation with multifinger hands, micro/nano systems, sensor information, multimodal interface and human robot interaction, and robot vision.

As peer-reviewed archival publications, all accepted papers will be hosted on IEEE Xplore. Prospective authors are invited to submit high-quality papers representing original work. Submissions in all areas of intelligent robots and applications are welcome.

### KEY DATES

### PREPARING YOUR SUBMISSION

Papers should be up to eight pages (US letter size) in length. The number of pages includes the references, appendices, etc. This replaces the old 6+2 model, and there is no option to pay for additional pages.

#### Note

IROS no longer offers an RA-L+IROS submission option. All published papers from RAM and non-evolutionary published RA-L, T-ASE and T-RO papers are eligible for presentation at IROS, during an eligibility time window.

IEEE RAS has also implemented a [reviewer/author tracking system in PaperCept](https://www.ieee-ras.org/new-reviewer-author-balance-tracking-system-for-ieee-ras-publications/) from January 1, 2026. Authors are strongly encouraged to provide high-quality reviews for the success of IROS 2026.

### Video Supplement

A 1-minute video (up to 10 MB) can be added to supplement your paper. Videos are not mandatory; rather, it’s an opportunity to augment one’s paper to visibly showcase results. MPG, MPEG or MP4 video formats can be used. One can upload or re-upload the video attachment no later than March 5, 2026 (23:59 PST).

### Show and Tell

IROS is testing a new “Show and Tell” option for papers, in which authors of some accepted papers (in addition to their standard presentation and poster) will have the opportunity to present a short, self-supported demonstration of a physical robot (or component). If you would like to be considered for a slot in the Show and Tell track, please check the box to indicate this on PaperCept and provide a brief description of what you would show.

### Resubmission of Papers Rejected from ICRA 2026

IROS 2026 has a submission category “ICRA-IROS transfer” for papers that previously were submitted to ICRA 2026, but were not accepted for publication. The submission should include an additional Author Response File (PDF) to clarify how the comments from the AE/Editor and the reviewers were taken into account in the preparation of the revised paper. The papers will undergo a regular review process.

It is authors’ responsibility to **not** identify themselves in the response file.

Author responses may be full rebuttals, but may also be shorter documents highlighting changes you have made to the paper in response to the reviewer comments. This response gives you as an author the opportunity to aid the reviewers in providing a fair and useful assessment of your paper.

For example, if you believe the reviewer misunderstood the context of your paper, you could update the introduction to more directly situate the reader, and then note in your author response that a question was raised about the context and that you have clarified this point in the introduction.

### Review Process

The IROS Conference Paper Review Board rigorously reviews all submissions and then through the IROS 2026 Senior Program Committee, renders acceptance decisions.

The IROS review process is double-anonymous (both reviewers and authors stay anonymous). Therefore, manuscripts should not include author names or their affiliations.

Best Paper Awards in various categories are also selected by their respective committees.

#### Expectation of In-Person Presentations

All papers accepted at the 2026 IROS are to be presented, in person, by the author/co-author or a knowledgeable person on the subject matter.

### Guidelines  for Generative AI Usage

Generative AI tools and technologies, such as ChatGPT, may not be listed as authors of a paper submitted to IROS 2026. The use of content generated by AI in an article (including but not limited to text, figures, images, and code) must be disclosed in the acknowledgments section.

The AI system used must be identified, and specific sections of the article that use AI-generated content should be specified and accompanied by a brief explanation regarding the manner in which the AI system was used to generate the content.

Information or content contained in or about a manuscript under review shall not be processed through an AI system. Doing so is considered a breach of confidentiality and as such is not permitted.
