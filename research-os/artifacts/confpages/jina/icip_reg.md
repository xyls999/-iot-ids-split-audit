## **Conference Registration Benefits**

* * *

**Author Full Conference –** Access to all technical sessions, as well as the welcome reception, breaks, exhibits, co-located workshops, electronic proceedings, farewell music party and a conference gala ticket. Registration may cover up to four papers (main conference & workshops combined) in which the registrant appears in the author list. All papers must be presented in-person.  
_Please note that tutorials, short courses, and additional activities incur an additional fee._

**Non-Author Full Conference** – Access to all technical sessions, as well as the welcome reception, breaks, exhibits, co-located workshops, electronic proceedings, farewell music party and a conference gala ticket.  
_Please note that tutorials, short courses, and additional activities incur an additional fee._

**Workshop Only (without Conference Registration)** – Access to all workshops only on the selected day, including breaks, & access to the workshop electronic proceedings. A Sunday workshop ticket includes access to the Welcome Reception. A Thursday workshop ticket includes access to the Farewell Music Party on Wednesday evening. To attend workshops for multiple days, you need to register for each day separately.  
_Please note: if you registered for the conference, free access to workshops is included. Main conference author registration is required to cover workshop papers (up to four total, including main conference & workshop papers)._

**Non-Author Conference One-Day Registration** – Access to all technical sessions on either Monday, Tuesday or Wednesday including coffee breaks, lunch, exhibit, electronic conference proceedings, and access to the Farewell Music Party on Wednesday evening.  
_Please note that registrations for student and young professional events include an additional cost._

* * *

All registration fees are subject to a VAT rate of 25.5%.
