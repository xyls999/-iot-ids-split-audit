## Key Information to Register

### Registration for Authors

Authors have received their paper acceptance emails on 31 January 2026.   
Authors who have not registered by 31 March 2026 (incl. payment) or papers exceeding the (8) page limit (excluding accepted RA-L papers with ICRA option, TRO, and RAM papers) in the final submitted paper version, will have their paper removed from the program and the proceedings.

**Please note:**  
All authors can register and pay. **Only after the registration is fully paid, the Papercept upload code for the final paper will be provided.**

_Please observe the author registration deadline being 31 March 2026, 23:59:00 EST._

### Registration for non-authors

All Early Registrations must be completed, and payment received by 31 March 2026 (23:59:00 EST) to incur the early bird rates.   
After this date, the rates will increase as per the table below.

Attention: Make sure to settle your IEEE and/or RAS membership BEFORE registering!   
(You cannot claim the lower member registration fee once you have already settled your registration fee!)  
Please allow some hours between paying your membership and start of your registration.   
(The membership system and the registration system must have time to sync…)

### Registration for COMPETITION PARTICIPANTS

Active competition participants can contact their competition organizer for further information on the special **Competition Only Registration**.

#### Notes

1.   **Paper Uploads:** To submit the final paper and all associated attachments, at least one of the authors must register in advance (see above). Authors who pay a non-student registration fee (excludes one-day registrations) are allowed to upload up to 2 contributions, and all other registrations are limited to one paper upload (excludes one-day registrations). Papers that do not respect the (8) page limit (excludes accepted RA-L papers with ICRA option, TRO, and RAM papers) in the final submitted paper version will be removed from the program and the proceedings. No exceptions will be made. One regular registration can be used to upload no more than two papers and all associated attachments. If you are an author or co-author of a single contribution, your registration cannot be shared or used for uploading a paper for which you are not an author. One student registration can be used for upload of only one paper.
2.   **IEEE RAS and IEEE Student Member:**To register as a student, select the appropriate student registration category during the online registration.
3.   **1-day Passes:** Are valid only on the main conference days (2-4 June 2026).
4.   Attending both, the main conference and the workshops/tutorials requires registration for both. **Access to the main conference and workshops/tutorials will be strictly controlled, and only delegates with valid badges/passes will be allowed entry to the respective areas.**
5.   **Networking events:**Refers to Welcome Reception (Monday, 1 June) and (_if included in the ticket type_) the Imperial Night (Wednesday, 3 June)

### Method of Payment

Registration can only be confirmed once payment is received.

Payment can be made by any of the following methods:

*   Credit card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
*   Wire payment

### Cancellation Policy

Written cancellation will only be accepted **until 11 April 2026** for a full refund, less a EUR 50 cancellation fee. Refunds for cancellations received **after 11 April 2026** will only be made in exceptional circumstances. In case of failure to obtain the appropriate visa to travel to Austria, please [contact us.](mailto:ICRA2026registration@aimgroup.eu)

Please note, you need to retain the registration of at least one author to make sure your ICRA paper will be published. The name on the registration can be changed without penalty.

All best efforts will be made to present the program as published on the Conference website. The Organizing Committee and the Conference Organizers are not liable for any loss or inconvenience caused as a result of such alteration. The Conference does not cover individuals against cancellations of bookings or theft or damage to belongings.
