### NOTES

1.   **Paper Uploads:**To submit the final paper and all associated attachments, at least one of the authors must register in advance (see above). Authors who pay a non-student registration fee (excludes one-day registrations) are allowed to upload up to 2 items from these categories: paper, late-breaking submissions, standalone video. All other registrations include one submission of the same categories (excludes one-day registrations). Papers should be up to eight pages (US letter size) in length. The number of pages includes the references, appendices, etc.Registrations cannot be shared or used for uploading a paper for which the registrant is not an author.
2.   **IEEE Student Member:**To register as a student, select the appropriate student registration category during the online registration.
3.   **1-day Passes:** Are valid only on the main conference days (September 28–30).
4.   Attending both the main conference and the workshops/tutorials requires registration for both.  
**Access to the main conference and workshops/tutorials will be strictly controlled, and only delegates with valid badges/passes will be allowed entry to the respective areas.**
