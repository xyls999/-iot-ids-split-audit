## An Invitation to Contribute

The ICRA 2026 Organizing Committee invites researchers, academics, industry practitioners, and the general public to submit papers, host competitions, and present workshops and tutorials that capture the present and the future of robotics.

### Call for Papers

**About:**ICRA 2026 will bring together the world’s top researchers and leading companies to share ideas and advances in Robotics and Automation. Researchers and the general public are encouraged to submit papers that capture the present and the future of robotics. Submissions are due by 15 September 2025.

**Submission Information:**[View submission details here.](https://2026.ieee-icra.org/call-for-icra-2026-papers-now-accepting-submissions/)

**Important Deadlines:**

*   **Paper Submission Deadline:** 15 September 2025 (11:59 PST)
*   **Submission of accompanying videos:** Open (1) Aug 5-Sept 9, 2025, and (2) Sept 17-22, 2025 (11:59 PST).

**Contact Information:**For any questions, please contact: [icra2026tp@ieee.org](mailto:icra2026tp@ieee.org).

### CALL FOR WORKSHOPS/TUTORIALS

**About:**ICRA workshops/tutorials focus on emerging topics in robotics, including new problems, new domains, and robotics in emerging economies, and tutorials on the fundamentals of robotics for newcomers to the field. Proposals are evaluated based upon the topic, the proposed event’s format and likelihood of creating an engaging intellectual environment, and the inclusiveness of the proposed event.

**Submission Information:**[View submission details here.](https://2026.ieee-icra.org/call-for-icra-2026-workshops-tutorials-proposals/)

**Important Deadlines:**

*   **Submission Deadline:**September 25th, 2025
*   **Acceptance Notification:**December 21st, 2025

**Contact Information:**For any questions, please contact the ICRA Workshop and Tutorials Co-Chairs: [icra2026wt@ieee.org](mailto:icra2025wt@ieee.org).
