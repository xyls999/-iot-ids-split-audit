### RESUBMISSION OF PAPERS REJECTED FROM IROS 2025

ICRA 2026 introduces a new submission category “**IROS-ICRA transfer**” for papers that previously were submitted to IROS 2025, but were not accepted for publication. The submission should include an additional Author Response File (PDF) to clarify how the comments from the AE/Editor and the reviewers were taken into account in the preparation of the revised paper. The papers will undergo a regular review process.

It is authors’ responsibility to **not** identify themselves in the response file.

It is also the authors’ responsibility to download reviewers’ comments for the rejected paper and make it a part of author responses.

### Presentation of RA-L, RAM, T-ASE, T-RO and T-FR papers

All published papers from RAM and non-evolutionary published RA-L, T-ASE, T-RO and T-FR papers are eligible for presentation at ICRA, during an eligibility time window. Authors are automatically offered the option to present their paper at one of these conferences upon notification of final acceptance. Please, check the webpage of the journals for information on eligibility time window: [RA-L](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ra-l),[RAM](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/ram),[T-ASE](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/t-ase), [T-RO](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/t-ro) and [T-FR](https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/t-fr). The **deadline for transfer** of journal papers to ICRA 2026 is**31 st December 2025**.

### For Authors: Guidelines regarding the use and disclosure of AI tools

Generative AI tools and technologies, such as ChatGPT, may not be listed as authors of a paper submitted to ICRA 2026. The use of content generated by AI in an article (including but not limited to text, figures, images, and code) must be disclosed in the acknowledgments section. The AI system used must be identified, and specific sections of the article that use AI-generated content should be specified and accompanied by a brief explanation regarding the manner in which the AI system was used to generate the content. The use of AI systems for editing and grammar enhancement is common practice and, as such, is generally outside the intent of the above policy.

Information or content contained in or about a manuscript under review shall not be processed through an AI system. Doing so is considered a breach of confidentiality and as such is not permitted.

**If your paper is found to be in violation of IEEE-RAS AI use policies and is subsequently disqualified or withdrawn from the conference program, your registration fees will NOT be refunded.**

### FAQ

Traditionally, ICRA covers all areas of robotics research. In order to find out if your paper will fit to the conference, you may want to check the ICRA keywords. When you submit a paper, you should select at least three keywords from this list.

Is the ICRA review process single-blind or double-blind?

The ICRA review process is double-anonymous (both reviewers and authors stay anonymous). Therefore, manuscripts should exclude the authors and their affiliations. However, when all the co-authors must be entered in Paperplaza at the time of first submission. The author information will be visible to the editors and AEs only, but not to reviewers.

You can find templates here:[Important Information for Manuscript Preparation (papercept.net)](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https:/ras.papercept.net/conferences/support/support.php__;!!DZ3fjg!7azbR7XjKgBx1OmbmUmGwkG3z6zbDHJ876mSfMlOPccKdT3cFaAZeq220xtwP0cSJY1etMJWNQidY1-Qr5gjfxaz5VadWQ$)

Can I add supplementary material in the paper or as an attachment?

The allowed paper length is 8 pages, meaning 8 pages for the complete paper content including all material and references. Any supplementary material must fit within the 8 regular pages. A video attachment is possible.

Will there be an extension to the paper submission deadline?

There is no plan to extend the paper submission deadline.

Can I add weblinks in the paper?

You can include URLs (not links embedded in the pdf), but you should not expect that reviewers will look at those links, or that they will influence the review process. Beyond the submitted paper and the (optional) supplementary video, reviewers are not required to look at any other material when writing their reviews.

Please find more information of the PDF requirements on[Compliance (papercept.net)](https://urldefense.com/v3/__https:/ras.papercept.net/conferences/support/general.php__;!!DZ3fjg!70OeHvOG2LN1gbAqd2MvMLbknzqEtKvzqNWSOkdh2eu8AEMdlP6BAJ-dndefa6mAgg05N-YWQPqv-ueL335arp3OTzQ8Gg$).

Is it allowed to submit a paper that previously was published at a workshop or on a preprint server (e.g. arxiv)?

IEEE supports the evolutionary publication paradigm of workshop papers being improved to conference papers, which are then improved to journal papers. Therefore, papers, which previously have been published at a workshop without formal/peer reviewed proceedings, can be submitted to ICRA. Similarly, papers, which have been uploaded on a preprint server (e.g. arxiv) and are not submitted simultaneously to any other conference or journal, can be submitted to ICRA.

However, if your workshop paper has a DOI, this would be considered as an archival publication equivalent to a conference paper. Such papers cannot be submitted.

How can I transfer an accepted paper from T-ASE/T-FR/RA-P for presentation at ICRA?

If you want to transfer an accepted paper from one of the journals T-ASE, T-FR, or RA-P to ICRA, the paper should be submitted in paperplaza in the relevant submission category of ICRA. Do not submit it as a regular ICRA paper! For the paper upload, you will need to enter a code, which can be obtained from the Editor-In-Chief of the journal.

Notice that papers from T-RO, RA-M, and RA-L follow a different process and do not require a code. Authors of accepted papers will receive an email from PaperCept regarding the transfer of their paper to a conference at which they wish to present their paper.

The deadline for all transfers of journal papers to ICRA 2026 is 30 November 2025.
